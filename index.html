About the game

Ashbound is a fast, one-thumb action roguelite. Every run takes you deeper into a fallen kingdom: dodge, strike, pick your upgrades and see how far the ash carries you.

Bosses

  • Fallen KnightHeavy blows and long wind-ups. Read them or fall.
  • Ash BishopChains, rituals and ground-shaking slams.
  • Dead ArcherA patient hunter who never misses twice.
  • Fallen KingLeaps, rushes and a shield that bites back.

Survival mode

Hold out in the old graveyard as the dead rise from their graves, the mist thickens and the night grows longer.
