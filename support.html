Support

Need help with Ashbound, found a bug or have a question? Open a support request and we will get back to you.

Open a support request

Requests are public on GitHub, so please do not include personal information. Mention your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened.

Frequently asked questions

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Progress is saved on your device. There is no account, so progress does not sync between devices, and deleting the app deletes it. A run in progress is also saved when you leave the app and offered back when you return.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Ads are optional. The only ad is a rewarded video that you can choose to watch to get a second chance after being defeated. The game is fully playable without watching any.

The “Watch ad” button is greyed out.

An ad is not loaded yet or none is available right now. Check your internet connection and try again in a moment.

How do I change my ad or tracking choices?

Tracking: open iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and switch Ashbound on or off. Consent message (EEA, UK, Switzerland, US states): Ashbound has no in-game button for it yet. Deleting and reinstalling the app resets your choices, and the consent message is shown again on the next launch (if it applies in your region). See the Privacy Policy.

How do I reset my progress?

Delete the app and reinstall it. This removes all local data.

What data does the game collect?

We collect none ourselves. Google AdMob processes some data to show ads; details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I report a bug?

Open an issue with your device model, iOS version, what you were doing and, if possible, the boss or stage.
